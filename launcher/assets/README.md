# Launcher artwork

`robot-blueprint.png` is original launcher artwork generated with the built-in
imagegen tool, independent of the original game's files. It is available before
the player supplies a disc. The service-bay direction was selected by the user
from three launcher concepts.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a production bitmap asset for the left
> sidebar of a desktop launcher for One Must Fall: Battlegrounds, a 2003 giant
> robot arena fighting game. Portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. A single original formidable
> bipedal arena combat robot drawn as a precise industrial technical schematic,
> three-quarter front view, entire robot visible from head to feet, big broad
> mechanical shoulders, articulated piston joints, heavy feet, compact torso,
> asymmetric industrial armor, no carried weapons. Warm thin ivory drafting lines
> on almost black graphite (#141718). Subtle construction grid and sparse dimension
> leader lines, no text, letters or numbers anywhere. Refined architectural
> engineering drawing with just a little muted amber accent on the chest.
> Completely flat graphic, no perspective presentation of the sheet. Robot
> occupies the middle 80 percent of height and 85 percent width; ample unobstructed
> dark top and bottom margins. Crisp intricate detailed wireframe lines with a
> quiet understated technical mood, readable silhouette. No blue, cyan, neon,
> glowing holograms, colorful illustration, logos, interface controls or framing
> border. This is the actual artwork only, not an app mockup.
