# Code of Conduct

We want participation in openomf-battlegrounds to be welcoming and respectful,
regardless of experience, identity or background. This applies to project issues,
pull requests and other project spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, assume good intent and respect people's boundaries.
- Give constructive feedback about the work and accept feedback in good faith.
- Make room for newcomers and differing viewpoints.
- Respect privacy and give credit for others' contributions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption and publishing someone's private information without
permission are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to Kadima Tech at
[jacobus@kadima-tech.com](mailto:jacobus@kadima-tech.com). For a public incident,
you can also flag the relevant comment with GitHub's reporting tools. Do not
post a confidential report in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports, respect the privacy of the people involved,
and take action appropriate to the situation. Actions may include requesting a
change, removing content, a warning, or a temporary or permanent participation
restriction. A maintainer involved in a report should step back from handling it.
You may ask another uninvolved maintainer to review an enforcement decision.
